I am an undergraduate student majoring in Statistics + Machine Learning at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/). I have been fortunate enough to learn about many different disciplines in my time here and my two years at the [University of North Texas](https://www.unt.edu) from world-class faculty and an incredible group of peers at CMU and [TAMS](https://tams.unt.edu/). While my main focus is in statistics, I am particularly interested in the applications of data science in the [natural environment](https://archithsharma.github.io/projects/cusum-portal/), [ecology](https://archithsharma.github.io/publication/2025-01-02-kestrel-decline-ebird-subsampling), [sports](https://archithsharma.github.io/publication/2025-04-04-stats-and-stumps-t20i-prediction), particularly with a focus on inferring causality.

Outside of my academic pursuits, I am a decent pianist and an [avid birder](https://ebird.org/profile/Mzc0NTM0Mw). I'm also a nerd about Nintendo games :)

I welcome all to reach out! Feel free to connect with me, and I welcome messages from strangers - the stranger the better!!! :)
